01 Who is responsible

Anluti Mall operates a website selling plush toys and toys, and an internal warehouse management app. This policy applies to accounts and data across these services. Contact: support@anlutimall.shop.

02 Data collected

  • Full name.
  • Email.
  • Avatar.
  • Password: only when registering by email, stored as a one-way hash that no one can read.
  • Google account identifier: when signing in with Google, we receive only your name, email and avatar — never your Google password, and no access to Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Email verification status.
  • Account creation time and last login time.

We do not collect bank card numbers.

03 Purposes of use

  • Account creation and sign-in.
  • Email verification and password reset.
  • Displaying name and picture in the account.
  • Managing employee permissions in the warehouse management application.

We do not sell data and do not use data for advertising.

04 Third parties

Third party Role Data received
Google Google sign-in Identity verification, name, email, avatar
Cloudflare R2 Image storage (product photos, avatars) Image files
Resend Sending verification and password reset emails Email address, email content

Beyond the parties above, we only disclose data when required by law.

05 Browser storage

The website stores login sessions, shopping carts, and wishlists in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking tools. You can clear this data by logging out or clearing site data in your browser.

06 Data retention

We retain data for as long as your account remains active. To delete your account, send an email from your registered address to the address in section 1; we will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Order records may be retained longer if required by accounting or tax laws.

07 Your rights

  • View and edit information on your account page.
  • Request a copy of your data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for Google sign-in (revoke permissions at myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Send requests by email to the address in section 1. We respond within 30 days.

08 Policy changes

When this policy changes, we update the effective date at the top of the page. Significant changes will be notified via email or on the website.
